(02) — Core principle

Maximum utilisation of existing resources.

Before reaching for more time, more headcount, or more capital, I ask what's already on hand — an audit lens applied to a treasury problem, a reconciliation automated with software already licensed, a second read on a portfolio no one thought to re-underwrite. But utilisation only compounds if the toolkit keeps expanding, so I stay curious by design — exploring finance, economics, technology and strategy beyond my brief, and closing every day having learned something beyond the obvious.
